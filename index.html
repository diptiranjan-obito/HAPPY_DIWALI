Dear Anamcara,

Tonight the whole city is lighting lamps, and I wanted to light one for you too, even if it's a little late.

Some words become special because of the people they remind us of. And some people make even the simplest words feel like they hold an entire universe. I suppose you're one of those rare people for me.

You make ordinary days feel warmer. The little things you notice, the way you make me laugh, the way you understand me without me having to explain. I don't say this enough, so I'm saying it now.

Kuch rishte khuda ki likhi khoobsurat nazm hote hain,

Na waqt unki ahmiyat ko kabhi kam kar paata hai,
Na faaslon se unka khoobsurat ehsaas mit paata hai.

May this year bring you everything you've been quietly hoping for, and may you always feel as cherished as you make me feel.

Happy Diwali. With endless fondness .
